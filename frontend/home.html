About Authority · 3D Bhu-Aadhaar

The Authority is a specialized 3D Cadastral & Vertical Property Authority engineered by Team AeroNerds for the Smart India Hackathon 2026 (SIH26011), in alignment with the Department of Land Resources (DoLR), Ministry of Rural Development. The platform pioneers 3D Bhu-Aadhaar and assigns discrete 3D spatial identifiers (3D ULPIN) to multi-storey buildings, vertical property units, and subterranean infrastructure across India. Height signals are derived from satellite DSM and DEM and never presented as an exact floor count without field verification. Statutory aspects such as legal title, tax dues, and encumbrance are formally declared not determinable (NOT_DETERMINABLE).

How does 3D Bhu-Aadhaar assign an identity to a multi-storey flat?
It fuses 2D Khasra parcels, Copernicus bare-earth DEM ground datum, and building footprint heights to calculate vertical floor boundaries and assign a hierarchical 3D ULPIN.
What is the difference between bare-earth DEM and surface DSM?
Bare-earth DEM captures ground elevation above Mean Sea Level (MSL), whereas DSM includes rooftop heights and vegetation canopy, allowing precise slab extraction.
How are FSI/FAR violations flagged for town planners?
The automated compliance scanner evaluates total constructed floor area against permitted municipal plot coverage and zone ceilings in real time.
Can a citizen dispute a vertical parcel boundary?
Yes. Citizens can lodge an official grievance directly to the Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) desk with supporting sanction plans and mutation proofs.

17-Phase Processing Pipeline

From raw KML vector extraction, polygon simplification, DEM ground datum sampling, and floor estimation to supervised AI anomaly gating (Isolation Forest), our automated pipeline prepares immutable 3D ULPIN registry manifests for municipal registrar approval.

1. Boundary Ingestion 2. Footprint Dissolve 3. Copernicus DEM Offset 4. Sentinel-2 NDVI Mask 5. Discrete Floor Slicing 6. 3D ULPIN Minting 7. Anomaly Gating 8. Registrar Ledger
