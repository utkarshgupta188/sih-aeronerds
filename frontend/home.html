About Department of Land Resources (DoLR)

The Department of Land Resources (DoLR), Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India, executes the Digital India Land Records Modernization Programme (DILRMP) and SVAMITVA Scheme.

Bhumi Adhaar (3D ULPIN) extends 2D cadastral boundary maps into 3D vertical spatial identities for multi-storey high-rise apartments, underground utility tunnels, and commercial structures without fabricating title deeds.

How does 3D Bhu-Aadhaar assign identities for multi-storey flats?
3D Bhu-Aadhaar fuses 2D cadastral Khasra survey plot outlines with 3D building extrusions, ground DEM elevation (m MSL), and floor slab height estimation to construct hierarchical floor-level spatial identifiers (e.g., IN-MP-BHP-P104-B3-FL4-U402).
What is DILRMP Rule 8 Statutory Adjudication?
Under DILRMP Rule 8, AI anomaly flags and spatial boundary overlaps are surfaced to the Chief Cadastral Surveyor & Revenue Registrar for statutory review with full audit logging (APPROVE, CORRECT, REJECT, UNRESOLVED).
Can a citizen view and verify their 3D Title Deed certificate?
Yes. Citizens can view their official 3D property passbook, download printable QR-coded 3D land title certificates, and verify ground elevation MSL profiles.
