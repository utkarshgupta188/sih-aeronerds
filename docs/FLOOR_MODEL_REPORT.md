## Limitations
- Labels are crowd-sourced OSM tags; some are wrong/approximate.
- Training data is greater Bengaluru, applied to the same city - low domain shift, but still a shift from the small AOI; the AOI check above quantifies it.
- No imagery features (no sub-metre imagery for these buildings).
- Predicts a floor *count* from footprint shape + OSM tags; genuinely ambiguous footprints are rejected rather than guessed.
